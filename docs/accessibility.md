# Accessibility

Q Intelligence is operated by Scott Harvey-Whittle trading as SHW Digital
Services. We want the website and app to be usable by people with different
access needs.

## Using Q

The public About, Contact and legal pages are readable without JavaScript.
The interactive app needs JavaScript. Public pages support browser zoom and
responsive layouts. Navigation uses labelled links and visible keyboard focus.
Charts on the Stored Events dashboard also provide text summaries and a table.

## Known limitations

Q has not been independently certified as meeting a particular accessibility
standard. AI responses, third-party payment and support interfaces, and complex
app controls may present barriers. Automated checks alone cannot establish full
accessibility. We continue to review keyboard, screen-reader and mobile use.

## Report a barrier

Email [office@q-ai.online](mailto:office@q-ai.online) with the page URL, what you
were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology if relevant. Please
do not include passwords or private journal content. Tell us the format or
adjustment that would help; we will discuss a practical alternative.
