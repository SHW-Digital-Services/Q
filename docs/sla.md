# Q Intelligence Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Public  
**Effective date:** 21 October 2026  
**Last reviewed:** 21 October 2026  
**Service owner:** Q Intelligence (SHW Digital Services)

## 1. Welcome

At Q Intelligence, we believe support should be clear, respectful and transparent. This Service Level Agreement (SLA) explains how we deliver customer support, communicate about service issues and measure our support objectives.

We aim to provide reliable support and clear communication while being honest about what we can and cannot guarantee. This SLA is intended for customers, partners and service users of Q Intelligence.

## 2. Purpose

This SLA describes:

- The scope of Q Intelligence customer support services.
- Support availability and operating hours.
- How incidents and support requests are prioritised.
- Response objectives and escalation routes.
- Maintenance and service communications.
- Security and privacy responsibilities.
- Service review and change management arrangements.

This document does not create service credits, financial penalties, warranties, guarantees or legally enforceable performance commitments unless expressly incorporated into a separate written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Service scope

Q Intelligence support provides assistance with:

- Product guidance and general enquiries.
- Technical support and troubleshooting.
- Account access and recovery guidance.
- Subscription and billing support routing.
- Privacy and security concerns.
- Incident reporting and service communications.

### Out of scope

This SLA does not cover emergency services, crisis intervention, clinical or healthcare services, legal or financial advice, custom consultancy unless separately agreed, or third-party products and services outside Q Intelligence's control.

## 6. Incident priority definitions

Requests may be assigned a priority level to help Q Intelligence prioritise support fairly and consistently.

### Priority 1 — Critical

Complete loss of service or inability for most users to access core platform functionality. Examples include the platform being unavailable, a major outage affecting most users, or core functions being inaccessible.

### Priority 2 — High

Major functionality is unavailable or significantly degraded. Examples include an important feature being unavailable or significant service degradation affecting multiple users.

### Priority 3 — Medium

Functionality is reduced where a workaround exists or user impact is limited. Examples include a non-critical feature issue or a performance issue affecting a small user group.

### Priority 4 — Low

Minor issues, cosmetic defects, enhancement requests, content corrections or general enquiries. Examples include typographical errors, visual defects, feature suggestions and general support enquiries.

## 8. Resolution and restoration

Q Intelligence aims to resolve issues as quickly as reasonably possible but does not currently publish guaranteed resolution or restoration targets. Resolution times may vary depending on incident severity, technical complexity, third-party dependencies, security considerations, customer response times and availability of required information.

## 9. Support requests

Customers may submit support requests through approved Q Intelligence support channels. Requests should include a clear description of the issue, steps already taken, device or browser details where relevant, the approximate date and time of occurrence, and any non-sensitive error messages.

To protect privacy and security, customers should not include passwords, access codes, payment credentials, private journal entries or other sensitive information.

## 10. Escalation process

If a matter cannot be resolved through normal support channels, customers may request escalation.

1. **Level 1 — Standard Support Request:** Initial review and handling through standard support processes.
2. **Level 2 — Customer Service Review:** Escalation for additional review and oversight. Contact [cs@q-ai.online](mailto:cs@q-ai.online).
3. **Level 3 — Executive Review:** Escalation to the Chief Executive Officer or nominated service owner where appropriate. Contact [office@q-ai.online](mailto:office@q-ai.online).

## 11. Planned maintenance

Q Intelligence does not operate a fixed maintenance window. Where reasonably practicable, planned maintenance will be communicated in advance.

Emergency maintenance may be undertaken without advance notice where required to protect service integrity, address security concerns, maintain privacy safeguards, restore platform stability or protect customers and the service.

## 12. Incident communications

Service notices, incident updates and maintenance communications will be published through the [Q Intelligence Status Page](https://q-intelligence.betteruptime.com/en). Customers are encouraged to consult it for current service information and incident communications.

Q Intelligence currently provides incident notifications, service notices and maintenance communications where appropriate. Routine customer performance reports are not currently published.

## 13. Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for maintaining accurate account and contact information; protecting passwords, recovery links and account credentials; providing sufficient information to enable investigation; maintaining access to the registered email account associated with their service; promptly reporting suspected security concerns or unauthorised account access; and using Q Intelligence services in accordance with applicable terms and policies.

## 14. Security and privacy responsibilities

Q Intelligence is committed to handling information respectfully, responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

### Q Intelligence responsibilities

Q Intelligence will process information in accordance with its published Privacy Policy; request only information necessary to investigate or respond to support requests; restrict access to authorised personnel where required for legitimate operational purposes; investigate suspected security incidents through established internal procedures; and apply security and privacy controls appropriate to the service and associated risks.

### Customer responsibilities

Customers should keep account credentials secure; avoid sharing passwords, recovery links or authentication codes; report suspected compromises promptly; and provide accurate information when requesting support.

## 15. Service continuity

Q Intelligence maintains operational procedures intended to support service continuity and incident recovery. These arrangements may include platform monitoring, incident management processes, operational recovery procedures, and backup and continuity planning where appropriate.

Q Intelligence does not currently publish Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) or Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs).

## 16. Review and change management

This SLA is reviewed at least annually and following material changes to the service, operating model, legal requirements or customer support arrangements. Changes may reflect service improvements, regulatory requirements, security enhancements, operational changes or customer feedback. The most current version will be published through official Q Intelligence channels.

## 17. Service Level Agreement notice

This SLA describes the operational objectives, customer support arrangements and service-management practices used by Q Intelligence. Unless expressly incorporated into a separate written agreement signed by both parties, it does not create service credits, financial penalties, warranties, guarantees or legally enforceable performance commitments.

All service targets represent operational objectives pursued on a reasonable-endeavours basis and remain subject to operational, legal, privacy, security, safety and service-continuity considerations. Q Intelligence reserves the right to amend this SLA following service reviews, operational changes, legal requirements or updates to customer support arrangements.
