# Community Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

These Community Guidelines explain the standards of behaviour expected from everyone using **Q Intelligence**, operated by **Scott Harvey-Whittle trading as SHW Digital Services**.

Our goal is to foster a respectful, safe, inclusive, and productive environment for all users.

These Guidelines complement, but do not replace, the:

- Terms of Service
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Privacy Policy
- AI Disclaimer

---

# 2. Respect Others

When using Q Intelligence, you should:

- communicate respectfully;
- engage constructively;
- respect differing viewpoints;
- avoid abusive or discriminatory behaviour;
- treat other users with courtesy.

Healthy disagreement is welcome, but personal attacks are not.

---

# 3. Lawful Use

Use Q Intelligence only for lawful purposes.

You must not use the Service to:

- commit fraud;
- facilitate criminal activity;
- violate applicable laws;
- infringe intellectual property rights;
- evade legal obligations.

---

# 4. Responsible AI Use

Artificial intelligence should be used responsibly.

Users should:

- verify AI-generated information;
- apply human judgement;
- avoid presenting AI output as verified fact without review;
- disclose AI involvement where appropriate.

Do not use AI to generate content intended to deceive, manipulate, or harm others.

---

# 5. Respect Intellectual Property

Only upload or share material that you:

- own;
- are authorised to use;
- have permission to distribute.

Respect the copyrights, trademarks, privacy, and confidential information of others.

---

# 6. Protect Privacy

Respect the privacy of other individuals.

Do not upload, disclose, or share personal information unless you are authorised to do so and doing so complies with applicable law.

---

# 7. Prohibited Behaviour

The following behaviour is prohibited:

- harassment;
- hate speech;
- threats;
- intimidation;
- impersonation;
- fraud;
- distribution of malware;
- phishing;
- spam;
- deliberate misinformation intended to cause harm;
- unlawful or abusive content.

---

# 8. Security

Help protect the community by:

- safeguarding your login credentials;
- reporting suspected vulnerabilities responsibly;
- avoiding attempts to bypass platform security;
- reporting suspected abuse through the appropriate channels.

---

# 9. Enforcement

Where these Guidelines or our Terms are breached, we may:

- issue warnings;
- remove content;
- suspend accounts;
- permanently terminate accounts;
- report unlawful conduct where legally required.

Enforcement decisions will be made reasonably and proportionately.

---

# 10. Reporting Concerns

If you believe another user has violated these Guidelines, please contact us with:

- a description of the issue;
- relevant supporting information;
- links or screenshots where available.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably practicable.

---

# 11. Changes

These Community Guidelines may be updated to reflect:

- platform improvements;
- legal developments;
- security requirements;
- evolving community standards.

The latest version will always be available on **https://q-ai.online**.
