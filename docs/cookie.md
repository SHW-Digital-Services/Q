# 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

They allow websites to:

- remember user preferences;
- maintain authenticated sessions;
- improve security;
- analyse website usage;
- enhance functionality;
- improve performance.

Cookies may be:

- session cookies (deleted when your browser closes); or
- persistent cookies (stored until they expire or are deleted).

- delete cookies;
