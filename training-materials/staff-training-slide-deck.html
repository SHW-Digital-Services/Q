Supporting Users Securely

Operational Guidelines and Data Handling for Q Staff

The Golden Rules of Q Support

Never Copy Private Data

Private journal entries, chat logs, moods, or identity information must never be copied into our CRM notes, even for debugging.

No Passwords

Never ask for, or accept, user passwords or recovery links. We do not use credentials to verify identity over chat.

Payment Security

Raw card numbers and CVVs must never enter CRM fields. Direct users to the PayPal-hosted tools inside the application.

Secure Account Recovery

When a user cannot log in, staff must follow strict protocols to prevent social engineering attacks.

  • DO: Trigger the automated recovery link via the Supabase Auth system to the user's registered email.
  • DO NOT: Generate a link and paste it into a support chat or email manually.

Suspect a Breach?

If you suspect an account is compromised, freeze it immediately via the Admin portal and escalate the ticket.

Official Communications

All external support and security correspondence must follow uniform standards.

  • Group Email Only: All emails to users must be sent from, and directed to, the official group account: office@q-ai.online. Do not use personal staff emails.
  • Mandatory Signature: Ensure the official Q Intelligence signature is configured and appended to every outgoing email to maintain professional branding and trust.

When in doubt, Escalate.

If a request violates our Security Policy, or if you feel pressured by a user to bypass a protocol,

always escalate before taking action.
